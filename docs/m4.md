# M4 — Mortgage planning

Implemented on 2026-10-12. M4 supplies the app and shared calculation library. M1's full buyer profile and backend B4 are still pending.

## Delivered

- Mortgage dashboard with editable session inputs, a cash-and-income-constrained purchase budget, LTV limit, cash requirement, equity shortfall and payment-to-income indicator.
- Stable, Balanced and Flexible scenarios, track-mix donuts and a horizontal comparison screen. Each shows first payment, total repayments, interest and CPI additions separately.
- Plan details with loan, term, mix and individual rate controls; Spitzer or equal-principal repayment; CPI scenarios; and a one-percentage-point variable-rate stress illustration. Edits stay within the detail screen; the comparison continues to use the shared inputs.
- Victory Native principal/interest chart, SVG web counterpart, and a virtualized annual values view showing payments, principal, interest, indexation and outstanding balance.
- Cash-funded purchase tax, lawyer, agent, appraiser, advisor, moving and renovation costs. Fee percentages include any applicable VAT and can be edited.
- Property-specific mortgage card and price-prefilled calculator. English and Hebrew, logical spacing, numeric direction isolation, labeled form inputs and keyboard-operable web sliders.
- React Hook Form and Zod validation. Financial inputs stay in a non-persisted Zustand store: no financial data is written to localStorage, URLs or a server. A property price alone may appear in a calculator deep link. Clear resets the session.

## Calculation model

The shared code lives in `lib/domain/mortgage`, `lib/domain/tax` and `lib/domain/regulation`, following the backend specification's shared-domain layout. These modules have no RN, Expo, Node, I/O or internal-clock dependency. The caller supplies the tax date, rates and planning inputs. Amounts are ILS and annual rates are decimal fractions.

Annuity math uses `log1p` / `expm1` for stability near a zero rate. Schedules retain precision and round only for presentation; purchase tax rounds to agorot. The last payment clears the outstanding balance. CPI compounds monthly from an annual assumption, indexes the opening balance, and scales linked payments. Indexation is counted as added principal, separately from interest. Consequently, total repayments reconcile to original loan + interest + CPI additions. Future rate resets, index floors, insurance and early-repayment charges are not modeled.

Purchase costs are reserved from cash before computing the mortgage. The budget solver satisfies LTV, cash including tax/fees, and the app's 33% guideline for net income after the entered obligations. It uses the displayed fixed-unlinked illustrative rate and chosen term. Actual plan payments are calculated separately and can exceed that budget. All entered obligations are deducted conservatively; this is not a full bank underwriting model. An equity gap or an excessive loan is shown, never silently presented as approved.

## Rules and sources

- [Bank of Israel directive 329, June 2026 revision](https://www.boi.org.il/media/ez4npagt/329.pdf), sections 2, 5, 7 and 8: ordinary purchase LTV caps 75% / 70% / 50%, 50% payment-to-income cap, 66.66% variable-rate cap and 30-year maximum. [Circular 2852](https://www.boi.org.il/roles/supervisionregulation/circulars/cir2852/) is the version reference. The editor uses a conservative 34% fixed-unlinked minimum; four years is a product minimum, not a legal rule.
- [Israel Tax Authority instruction 1/2026](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/policy/inst-01-2026/he/realestate_inst-01-2026.pdf) supplies marginal sole/additional-home brackets. [Official simulator](https://www.gov.il/he/service/real_eatate_taxsimulator). The bundled configuration deliberately covers 2026 only, because the additional-home temporary provision expires on 2026-12-31. Dates outside that period stop estimates until configuration is updated.
- A replacement buyer defaults to additional-home tax; sole-home tax requires an explicit eligibility assumption. The app does not infer eligibility or sale deadlines. Special exemptions and nonresident cases are outside this calculator's scope.
- `lib/api/mortgage.ts` is the repository boundary for B4. Current inputs are **illustrative fallback rates**, not fetched live rates, current market averages, a bank quote or a recommendation. There is no fake backend connection or fabricated update timestamp.
